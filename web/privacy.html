Qisqacha

Tez Yodla — ingliz tili so'zlarini yodlash ilovasi. Biz faqat ilova ishlashi uchun kerakli ma'lumotni saqlaymiz: hisobingiz va o'rganish natijalaringiz. Ma'lumotlaringizni sotmaymiz, reklama uchun ishlatmaymiz va hech kimga bermaymiz.

Qanday ma'lumot saqlanadi

Ma'lumotNima uchun
Google hisobingiz: email, ism, Google identifikatoriHisobingizga kirish va progressni shu hisobga bog'lash
O'rganish natijalari: so'zlar, takrorlash jadvali, streak, sozlamalar, o'zingiz qo'shgan so'zlarProgressni saqlash va boshqa telefonda tiklash
Profil rasmi (agar o'zingiz qo'ysangiz)Profilda ko'rsatish. Rasm faqat sizga ko'rinadi
Ilova xatolari haqida anonim hisobot: xato matni, ekran nomi, telefon modeliIlovadagi nosozliklarni topish va tuzatish

Ism, so'zlaringiz va boshqa shaxsiy ma'lumot xato hisobotlariga qo'shilmaydi. Eslatmalar telefonning o'zida rejalashtiriladi.

Ma'lumot qayerda saqlanadi

  • Supabase — hisob va progress saqlanadigan bulutli baza. Har bir foydalanuvchi faqat o'z ma'lumotini ko'ra oladi.
  • Google — Google orqali kirish uchun.
  • Sentry — anonim xato hisobotlari uchun. Hisobotlar 90 kungacha saqlanadi.

Internetga yuboriladigan so'rovlar

O'zingiz yangi so'z qo'shganingizda ilova uning tarjimasi, ma'nosi va rasmini topish uchun shu so'zning o'zini quyidagi xizmatlarga yuboradi: MyMemory (tarjima), Free Dictionary API (lug'at), Unsplash (rasm). Bu so'rovlarga hisobingiz yoki boshqa shaxsiy ma'lumot qo'shilmaydi.

Ma'lumotni o'chirish

Hisobingizni va barcha ma'lumotlaringizni istalgan payt o'chirishingiz mumkin: ilovada Sozlamalar → Hisobni o'chirish. Ilova telefoningizda bo'lmasa, bu sahifadagi yo'l bilan so'rov yuboring.

O'chirilganda hisobingiz va bulutdagi progressingiz butunlay yo'q qilinadi va ularni qaytarib bo'lmaydi.

Bolalar

Ilova 13 yoshdan kichik bolalar uchun mo'ljallanmagan va ulardan ataylab ma'lumot to'plamaydi.

English version

Summary. Tez Yodla is an English vocabulary app. We store only what the app needs to work: your account and your learning progress. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone.

Data we store. Your Google account email, name and Google ID (to sign you in); your learning progress — words, review schedule, streak, settings and words you added (to save and restore it on another phone); a profile photo, only if you set one (visible only to you); anonymous crash reports — error message, screen name and device model (to fix bugs). Your name and words are never included in crash reports. Reminders are scheduled on the device.

Where it is stored. Supabase (cloud database; each user can access only their own data), Google (sign-in), Sentry (anonymous crash reports, kept up to 90 days).

Network requests. When you add a new word yourself, the app sends that word only to MyMemory (translation), Free Dictionary API (definitions) and Unsplash (photos). No account or personal data is attached.

Deleting your data. In the app: Settings → Delete account. Without the app, follow the steps on this page. Deletion permanently removes your account and cloud progress.

Children. The app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
