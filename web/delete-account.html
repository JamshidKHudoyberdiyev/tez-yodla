Hisobni o'chirish

Tez Yodla hisobingiz va barcha ma'lumotlaringizni o'chirish

O'zbekchaEnglish

1-yo'l: ilovaning o'zida (eng tez)

  1. Tez Yodla ilovasini oching.
  2. Sozlamalar → «Hisob» bo'limi ostidagi Hisobni o'chirish ni bosing.
  3. Tasdiqlash uchun O'CHIRISH deb yozing va «O'chirish» tugmasini bosing.

Hisob darhol o'chiriladi.

2-yo'l: ilova bo'lmasa — email orqali

Ilovaga kirgan Google email manzilingizdan bizga xat yozing. Mavzu: «Hisobni o'chirish». Shu email bilan bog'langan hisobni 7 kun ichida o'chiramiz va javob yozamiz.

Email yozish

Nima o'chiriladi

  • Hisobingiz (Google orqali kirish bog'lanishi)
  • Bulutdagi barcha progress: so'zlar, takrorlash jadvali, streak, sozlamalar, o'zingiz qo'shgan so'zlar, profil rasmi

Hech narsa saqlab qolinmaydi. Faqat anonim xato hisobotlari (ismingiz va so'zlaringizsiz) 90 kun ichida o'zi o'chadi.

O'chirilgan hisobni qaytarib bo'lmaydi. Kerak bo'lsa, avval ilovada Sozlamalar → Zaxira nusxa oling.

English

In the app (fastest): open Tez Yodla → Settings → Delete account (under «Hisob») → type O'CHIRISH to confirm → tap «O'chirish». Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app: email us from the Google address you signed in with, subject “Delete account”: jamshidnocode@gmail.com. We delete the account linked to that email within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted: your account and all cloud data — words, review schedule, streak, settings, words you added and profile photo. Nothing is retained, except anonymous crash reports (without your name or words), which expire within 90 days. Deletion cannot be undone.
